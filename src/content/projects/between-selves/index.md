## A — THE SHOW

Thirteen to eighteen hundred, one room, free entry. Painting, photography and
film on the walls; a long table in the middle where none of it stayed on the wall.

## B — IDENTITY PASSPORT

Everyone got a blank passport at the door and spent the afternoon stamping it —
a page per station, filled in by hand. By five it was the thing people were
carrying around, not the thing they came to look at.

## C — HENNA

Thirty-five designs over five hours, drawn freehand onto whoever sat down.
Nothing was pre-planned — each one was worked out on the hand it was going on.

## D — FIVE HOURS

A hundred and fifty people through the room. The installation ended up the most
photographed thing at the festival, which was the point — the work was supposed
to leave with them.
